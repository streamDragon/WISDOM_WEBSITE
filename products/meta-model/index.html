Meta Model Gym

Better questions begin with better listening.

A practice environment based on the NLP Meta Model. It trains you to notice deletion, distortion and generalisation in language, ask more precise questions and turn vague intentions into clearer choices and actions.

Practice

Language becomes something you can examine.

Meta Model Gym combines pattern recognition with active exercises for questioning, reframing and clarifying meaning. It is built as a training and self-reflection tool, not as a diagnostic system or a replacement for therapy.
