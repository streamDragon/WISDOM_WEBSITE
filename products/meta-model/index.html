Meta Model Gym

Better questions begin with better listening.

A practice environment inspired by the NLP Meta Model. It trains users to notice deletion, distortion and generalisation in language, ask more precise questions and turn vague intentions into clearer choices and actions.

Practice

Language becomes something you can inspect.

The product combines pattern recognition with active exercises for questioning, reframing and clarifying meaning. It is a training and self-reflection tool, not a diagnostic system or a replacement for therapy.

Meta Model patterns

Practise recognising deletion, distortion and generalisation across detailed sub-patterns.

Question training

Learn which questions recover missing information and challenge vague assumptions.

From vague to actionable

Turn unclear intentions into first steps, conditions and alternatives.

The principle

What is said is only part of the picture.

The visual language of the product uses layers and structure to make hidden assumptions easier to notice. The goal is not to make conversation robotic, but to make language more precise when precision matters.

As the product develops, public examples and training flows can be added here without changing the overall site structure.
