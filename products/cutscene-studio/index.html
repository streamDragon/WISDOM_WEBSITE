WISDOM Cutscene Studio

Tell your AI the scene. Build it inside your game.

Cutscene Studio connects the assets already inside a Unity project with the AI assistant you prefer. Describe the cinematic scene you want, preview it, validate it against the real project, and publish it as an editable Unity Timeline.

From direction to Timeline

A film idea becomes editable game content.

The system sits between natural-language direction and the real Unity project. The visual below represents the core flow without exposing internal project names or development tooling.

Workflow

AI direction grounded in your actual project.

The tool gives AI structured knowledge about actors, animations, environments, effects and other cinematic capabilities already available in the project.

01

Scan

Map the cinematic assets and capabilities already in the Unity project.

02

Describe

Talk to the AI assistant you already use and describe the scene normally.

03

Preview

See the cinematic result before committing it to the scene.

04

Validate

Check that the requested characters, animations, effects and staging are real.

05

Edit

Publish to Unity Timeline and keep changing it manually.

The point

Your AI should know what your game can actually do.

General AI can invent a beautiful scene, but it does not automatically know which character, animation or effect exists in your Unity project.

Cutscene Studio creates the bridge between cinematic intent and real project assets, so generated scenes can become editable game content instead of disposable text.
