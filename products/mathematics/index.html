Visual Mathematics

Learn mathematics by seeing it move.

A visual learning environment for middle and high school students. Instead of treating a function as a line of symbols, students can change parameters, watch the graph respond, practise step by step and get immediate feedback.

Inside the product

Mathematics becomes something you can manipulate.

These are real screens from the application: the graph, the task, and the learning tools live together instead of being split across a textbook and a calculator.

How it works

From formula to something visible.

The app combines interactive graphs with guided exercises, progress tracking and a tutor. Students can also bring in a question and turn it into a visual plan for solving it.

Interactive graphs

Change parameters and immediately see how the function changes.

Guided practice

Work through questions in steps with feedback, hints and progress.

Visual question analysis

Type or scan a question and break it into a structured visual plan.

Built-in tutor

Ask for help while staying inside the learning flow.
